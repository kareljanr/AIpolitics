## Executive judgment

Cyclo-cross is culturally Flemish; Worlds would likely attract private sponsors, tickets, and TV. Public cash must prove **additionality** (event would not happen / would shrink without grant) and **net tax > grant + congestion**. Prestige subsidies routinely fail that test. €350k is “only” 18 Belasting-FTE — still 18 people working a full year for someone else’s podium.

---

## Steelman

1. Tourism spillover (hotels, F&B) in shoulder season.  
2. Soft power / Flanders CX brand.  
3. Private co-spend may dwarf grant.  
4. EventFlanders process as quality filter.
