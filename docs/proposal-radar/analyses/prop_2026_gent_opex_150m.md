## Steelman (proponent’s best case)

Gent’s own multi-year plan already says the easy years are over. The foreword, signed by the finance alderman, ties the 2026-2031 plan to a structural sanitisation of €120 million a year at cruising speed by 2028, about 12 percent of the net budget, and to two legal contours: a positive autofinancieringsmarge, and no cannibalising of investment by the operating account. On the city’s schema M2, that marge for gemeente and OCMW is still negative in 2026 (−€28.6 million) and 2027 (−€5.7 million), turns positive in 2028 (+€25.0 million), and reaches about €50.1 million in 2031, which the toelichting calls roughly 3 percent of exploitatie-ontvangsten. That is a thin shock absorber, not a hoard.

Four months into the plan the absorber was already thinner. The college’s budget control of 30 April 2026 says a worse inflation path hits the 2031 marge by −€17 million through the wage bill, that the VVSG sectoral agreement pushes wages up further, and that the 2031 marge falls to €25 million. The same note says the 2025 operating account closed with a surplus of €85.68 million and that the city borrowed €48.36 million less than planned. The problem is not “the city cannot pay this month’s bills.” The problem is that the end-of-plan buffer the sanitisation was built to create is being used up before the sanitisation has landed.

Then the higher governments moved. On 30 September Peeters put the Gemeentefonds hit, on what the city then knew, at about €42 million a year in time, and the all-in extra effort, with inflation, interest and energy, at about €60 million a year (VRT) or €65 million (AVS the next day). That sits on top of the €120 million track, not instead of it. Federal tables were still open, including the pass-through of time-limited unemployment into the OCMW, which the MJP foreword already flags as a local overload. A college that waits for a perfect number will write the next budget amendment after the hole has already eaten the marge.

The October move is a search, and the city says so. The exact amount is not known, so services are asked for a wide menu: €150 million a year off the exploitatie, and up to €400 million less investment from 2027 to 2038. Peeters’ line, via VRT, is that the full amount will not be taken if it is not needed, that the point is to be able to choose, and that choices come in the spring, not before the end of the year. Taxes are possible and not previewed. Staff impact of this round is explicitly not promised either way. An oversized menu is how you avoid discovering in March that the only remaining options are a hasty tax or a broken autofinancieringsmarge. The alternative, pretending the September €60 million figure is the last word while federal decisions are still dark, is how a city misses the BBC equilibrium test in the last year of the plan.

## 1. Problem claim

- **Metric / baseline:** Three layers, and they are not the same number. (1) Adopted MJP 2026-2031, gemeente + OCMW: exploitatie-uitgaven €1,408.9 million in 2026; autofinancieringsmarge −€28.6 million (2026), −€5.7 million (2027), +€25.0 million (2028), +€50.1 million (2031). Net investment saldo about −€1.01 billion over 2026-2031 (sum of the M2 investeringssaldi). Gemeentefonds booked on schema T2 at €498.0 million in 2026, of which the basisdotatie is €441.3 million, rising on the basisdotatie by about 3.5 percent a year to €524.0 million in 2031. (2) Budget control 30 April 2026: 2031 marge revised to €25 million; inflation outlook −€17 million on that marge via wages; sectoral agreement extra, not split out. (3) The instrument under score: a search ceiling of €150 million a year on operating costs, and up to €400 million less investment from 2027 to 2038, because the exact higher-government hit is still unknown.
- **Who is hurt:** Gent residents, if core services or the maintenance of roads, parks and buildings are thinned to fill a transfer gap. City staff, already inside a sanitisation that VRT says removes about 400 functions over time. Local associations and the OCMW caseload, if subsidies and leefloon pressure arrive together. Taxpayers in Gent, if the residual is a local tax the MJP had frozen. Flemish and federal budgets are the other side of the transfer; this memo does not score those decisions.
- **Type:** state failure on the vertical transfer (a six-year local plan, then a change in the Gemeentefonds and an unfinished federal table) plus a macro cost shock (wages, energy, interest). Also a transfer fight about who holds the buffer. Not a claim that Gent has no preference problem in what it subsidises.
- **Truth of problem diagnosis (0–10):** **7.5**
- **Confidence:** **Strong** on the MJP marge path, the €120 million track, the Gemeentefonds path as booked, and the April revision of the 2031 marge to €25 million. **Medium** that Peeters, in late September, estimated about €42 million a year from the Gemeentefonds and about €60–65 million a year all-in. **Weak** that the right search size is €150 million a year and €400 million of investment: that pair is a staff note via one press outlet, and the alderman says the full operating number will not be taken if it is not needed.

## 2. Mechanism

Named mechanism(s): **budget constraint** under the BBC equilibrium rules (non-negative beschikbaar budgettair resultaat each year; non-negative autofinancieringsmarge in the last plan year). The city’s tools are a lower exploitatie, a lower investment path, and, still undecided, local tax or retributie. The October act is not yet any of those cuts. It is an **internal search** for a menu larger than the estimated hole.

Does the instrument actually attack that mechanism?

A real hole in the marge is closed only by higher receipts, lower operating spend, or lower debt service. Less net investment does not by itself repair the autofinancieringsmarge, which the city’s own leeswijzer defines as exploitatiesaldo minus net periodic repayments. It does change the loan path, and therefore later repayments, and it changes the capital stock. Using an investment haircut as if it were an operating saving would miss the constraint the college says it is protecting. The MJP foreword’s other contour was to avoid cannibalisation between investment and exploitatie, and it said almost 70 percent of the net €1 billion investment was maintenance of existing infrastructure, with historically high budgets for roads and green. A ceiling of €400 million less investment from 2027 to 2038 can honour that rule or break it. Twelve years inclusive is arithmetic only: €400 million / 12 ≈ €33 million a year if spread flat. The city has not published that split. Against net investment saldi of about €146–190 million a year in 2027-2031, a flat €33 million is a real slice, not a rounding error, and a front-loaded slice would be larger.

The operating ceiling does attack the right saldo, if what is cut is recurring exploitatie and if the euro taken is near the true gap. €150 million is about 10.6 percent of 2026 exploitatie-uitgaven (€1,408.9 million), on top of a €120 million track already inside the plan. Peeters’ September all-in figure was about €60 million (VRT) or €65 million (AVS), roughly two and a half times smaller than the ceiling. The city calls the exercise overgedimensioneerd and says the surplus above the true gap is not meant to be cut. That is a management technique. It is not yet a mechanism. A mechanism would rank overhead, subsidies and vacant posts against maintenance and the services the MJP said it would protect (safety, education, childcare, poverty, care, cleanliness, roads and green), and it would stop when the reconciled gap is closed.

**mechanism_fit (0–10):** **6.0**

## 3. Options considered

| ID | Option | One-line effect |
|----|--------|-----------------|
| A | Status quo | Keep the MJP, including the €120 million track and the April buffer already cut to €25 million in 2031, and absorb the Gemeentefonds and federal hits when they happen to land. |
| B | Abolish / deregulate | Drop the new search and also unwind local add-on tasks and subsidies the city is not obliged to run, instead of a second across-the-board percentage. |
| C | Price / tax reform | Move the three main local taxes the MJP froze (aanvullende personenbelasting, opcentiemen onroerende voorheffing, algemene bedrijfsbelasting), or raise user charges, as a voted choice rather than a residual. |
| D | Conditional transfer | Publish the gap bridge (marge, fund, wages, energy, leefloon) and take operating cuts only until that bridge is closed, with maintenance ring-fenced. |
| E | Public provision / invest | Hold the MJP investment path, especially maintenance, and close the hole entirely on exploitatie and receipts. |
| F | Pilot | Ask departments for a ranked €60–65 million menu first, the September all-in range, and open a second round only if the federal tables widen the hole. |
| G | **This proposal** | Search for €150 million a year of operating cuts and up to €400 million less investment in 2027-2038, choose in spring, and do not promise that the ceiling will be used. |

A is how the 2031 buffer goes from €50 million to €25 million to whatever the fund cut leaves, while the college still says the plan is in equilibrium. B is the abundance option inside the city’s own competence: stop doing what a municipality need not do, rather than thinning the capital stock. C is available and was explicitly rejected for the three main taxes in the MJP foreword; reopening it without a separate vote hides a tax rise inside a “savings” headline. D is this search with a stop-rule and a maintenance fence. E protects the capital stock and puts the whole adjustment on the operating account and on taxes, which is harsher for current services and cleaner for the marge definition. F matches the city’s own sentence that the full €150 million is not the goal. G without D’s stop-rule is a number that staff and unions will treat as a target, because that is how a €150 million internal note behaves.

## 5. Distribution & transfer constraint

- Winners: Gent’s future operating budget, if a smaller recurring gap is actually closed and the 2031 marge is rebuilt. Flemish and federal budgets, to the extent they have shifted a bill downward and the city absorbs it instead of sending it back as a political claim. Managers who get a menu before a vote.
- Losers: Staff, if this round repeats the earlier function cuts; ABVV already reads the ceiling that way. Residents, if cleanliness, childcare, education, safety or the OCMW offer is what remains after internal cuts fall short. Users of roads, parks and buildings, if the €400 million is maintenance rather than new prestige projects. Associations that live on municipal subsidies. The incidence is not published, so the loser list is the risk, not a decided distribution.
- Exit / entry risk for productive base: A local tax rise on the bedrijfsbelasting, or a visible drop in maintenance and safety, is the channel that touches firms and residents who can choose a neighbouring municipality. Nothing in the October note chooses that channel. The MJP had frozen APB, OOV and ABB and shifted some charges onto lodging and second homes. Reopening the tax door without a design is the exit risk to watch, not a fact already in the instrument.
- New permanent clientele? No. The instrument shrinks a budget. The capture channel is the opposite one: who is spared inside the menu.

**capture_risk (0–10):** **3.5**

## 7. Belgian competence & implementation

- Competent level: Stad Gent and OCMW Gent, under the decreet lokaal bestuur and the BBC equilibrium rules. The Gemeentefonds growth path is Flemish. Time-limiting unemployment, and the leefloon bill that follows, is federal. The city controls its exploitatie, its investment credits, and its own taxes. It does not control the shock.
- EU constraint: None specific to this search. State-aid and procurement rules still bind whatever is later cut or retendered. Not the issue in the staff note.
- Admin capacity / dual-structure friction: The €120 million track was designed to reach cruising speed by 2028 and to redraw the organogram. A second oversized round in October 2026, while that redraw is still starting (the April note says the MJP decisions had only just begun), asks the same organisation for another menu before the first one has a published outturn. City and OCMW are one planning perimeter in schema M2 and two political pressures on the ground. Autonomous agencies (sogent, District09, Kunsten en Erfgoed) sit in the consolidated marge and can absorb or dodge a city-only percentage.

**competence_fit (0–10):** **7.5**

## 9. Recommendation

`amend`

Minimum fix before the spring vote:

1. One bridge page: April 2031 marge (€25 million), Gemeentefonds delta against the booked basisdotatie path, wage and energy delta, and the federal leefloon delta, each with a source and a range.
2. Write the stop-rule into the staff instruction: the €150 million is a shelf of options, and the college stops when the bridge is closed. Do not let departments treat 10.6 percent as a quota.
3. Ring-fence maintenance of roads, parks and buildings, the share the MJP called almost 70 percent of net investment, from the €400 million haircut until the operating gap is closed. An investment cut is not an autofinancieringsmarge cut.
4. Show additionality against the €120 million track so this round is not the same euro counted twice.
5. If APB, OOV or ABB move, table that as its own decision. The MJP foreword froze them.
