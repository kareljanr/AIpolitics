## Executive judgment

Open-ended unemployment insurance in a high tax-wedge economy is a first-order **incentive design** problem, not a vibes problem. Time-limiting UI is one of the few Arizona measures that attacks a real mechanism (duration → search intensity → employment). Genius-class **direction**. Clown risk is mostly **fiscal theatre**: booking year-1 cash as if labour-market dynamics were instantaneous, or ignoring OCMW/CPAS substitution.

---

## Steelman (extensive)

1. **Problem is real.** Belgium combines top-of-OECD labour tax wedge with stubborn non-employment pockets. Long UI duration is repeatedly associated in labour economics with longer spells and lower exit hazards (search theory + empirical duration elasticities — **Medium/Strong** international evidence; Belgium-specific magnitudes **Medium**).

2. **Insurance ≠ permanent wage replacement.** Social insurance’s efficient form is temporary consumption smoothing + job search, not a parallel lifestyle income. A hard stop (with safety-net residual for those who *cannot* work) restores category honesty.

3. **Activation complementarity.** Time limits without job-search enforcement can dump people into inactivity statistics elsewhere; with monitoring, sanctions, and placement capacity, the reform is a package.

4. **Fiscal + abundance channel.** Higher employment raises tax base and cuts benefit outlays **if** exits are to work, not to other transfers. Dynamic abundance > static ledger games.

5. **Fairness steelman.** Unlimited UI is regressive toward workers who never claim; it socialises the cost of weak search and of regions/sectors with chronic mismatch without fixing mismatch.

## Critique (extensive)

### 1. Problem claim — graded

| Claim | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| Long UI duration reduces non-employment risk | Strong (theory + cross-country) |
| Belgium needs higher employment rate for fiscal sustainability | Strong (NBB/IMF/Commission class) |
| Arizona design’s *parameters* (exact months, exceptions) are optimal | Speculative without microdata eval |

### 2. Mechanism fit — high, with leak paths

Primary mechanism: **finite horizon raises search intensity** before benefit exhaustion (spike in exits near end is classic).

Leak paths (must monitor):

| Leak | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Shift to illness / invalidity benefits | Transfers rename, cost remains |
| Shift to CPAS/OCMW living wage | Local finance + stigma; may still be better than unlimited UI if activation differs |
| Household specialisation / black work | Measured employment understates activity |
| Weak PES capacity | Time limit without matching → poverty + political reversal |

ING (2026): government assumes roughly **one-third** of exhausted claimants find work; CoA sceptical on *speed* of savings. That assumption is the fiscal honesty fulcrum.

### 3. Options

| ID | Option |
|----|--------|
| A | Status quo unlimited / long UI |
| B | **Time limit + activation (this)** |
| C | Cut replacement rates, keep duration |
| D | Negative income tax / in-work credit instead |
| E | Public employment guarantee |
| F | Regional pilots with different caps |

**B** beats A on incentives. **D** can dominate if labour supply at extensive margin is the binding constraint—but BE politics and admin make D hard. Prefer B now; keep D as medium-term tax-benefit rewrite.

### 4. Distribution

| Winners | Losers |
|---------|--------|
| Taxpayers; firms facing tighter labour supply; short-spell unemployed who re-enter faster | Long-term unemployed who cannot find work; some low-productivity workers facing real hardship at cliff |

Transfer constraint: **helps** productive base if employment rises. Must keep **incapacity-tested** residual (illness, disability) so the reform does not pretend all non-employed are shirkers.

### 5. Fiscal honesty

| Item | Confidence |
|------|------------|
| Direction of long-run fiscal effect if employment rises | Medium–Strong |
| Year-1 static savings as booked in budget | **Weak** (CoA/ING) |
| Net general-government effect after CPAS | Weak until data |

**Do not** put a fake Belasting-FTE on a speculative billion.

### 6. Competence

Federal SS — correct. Regions own activation pieces (VDAB/FOREM/Actiris) — **coordination risk**.

### 7. Evidence

- Search/duration literature: Strong  
- BE implementation details & CoA: Medium  
- Dynamic employment elasticities BE-specific: Medium/Weak

## Recommendation

**`support`**, with mandatory:

1. Publish cohort exit destinations (work / CPAS / illness / other) quarterly.  
2. Do not book full steady-state savings in year 1.  
3. Protect true incapacity; ruthlessly activate the rest.  
4. Pair with labour-tax wedge cuts when fiscal space appears (convert employment gains into competitiveness).

**Falsifier:** employment exit rates of long-term unemployed do not rise within 3 years **and** other assistance absorbs ≥ savings 1:1 with no net employment gain.
