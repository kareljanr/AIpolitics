## Executive judgment

Entrepreneurship and dual training can have positive externalities — **if** outcomes beat cheaper providers. Here we have a clear euro figure and a reform narrative (central steering, efficiency, governance) but **no public unit costs, placement rates, or deadweight vs private training**. Fiscal honesty on the *input* is good; honesty on *output* is not. Mid scores correct: not circus, not genius.

---

## Steelman

1. **SME skills matter.** Flanders’ productive base needs continuous craft/entrepreneur training.  
2. **Network continuity.** Abrupt zero-funding during reform destroys capacity; transition year is rational.  
3. **Governance reform promise.** More cooperation, central steering, less duplication — if real, PV of reform could exceed one-year grant.  
4. **Political economy.** Training networks are quasi-public infrastructure with local presence.

---

## Critique

### Ambition vs evidence

Official text: quality offer for entrepreneurship; transition while reforming network.  

| Missing for ROI | Why fatal for genius score |
|-----------------|----------------------------|
| € per trainee / per completion | Cannot compare to Syntra private alternatives |
| Employment / firm survival outcomes | “Quality” is unfalsifiable |
| Overhead % of €49.7m | Capture risk in admin |
| What dies if grant = 0 | Additionality |

### Mechanism

Block grant → weak incentives unless multi-year contract with KPIs and clawbacks. “Reform while paying full freight” often means **pay now, reform later (never)**.

### Options

A full continue forever · B transition + published KPI gate (watch path) · C voucherize demand to any accredited provider · D cut 30% overhead first · E zero public / pure private.

Prefer **C/D** long-run; **B** acceptable only with hard 2028 evaluation kill switch.

### Distribution

Trainees and network staff win; general Flemish labour taxpayer pays **2,562 FTE-years of work-tax**.

---

## Recommendation

**`watch`.** Publish unit costs + placements within 12 months; no multi-year lock without KPIs.

**Falsifier:** reform cuts permanent overhead ≥15% by 2028 **and** placement/unit-cost beats voucher benchmark.
