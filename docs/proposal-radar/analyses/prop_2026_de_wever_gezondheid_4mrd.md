## Steelman (proponent’s best case)

The federal path through 2029 is still open, and health insurance is where a large part of the automatic growth sits. The kern had already, on Saturday 17 October, put the multi-year effort of €10 billion back on the table after partners killed a 2027-only fallback they called an attack on the index. A table that then asks the health budget to carry a defined share is the ordinary next sentence: an envelope with no composition is not yet a budget, and a budget that exempts the fastest statutory growth just pushes the residual back onto wages, benefits, or another tax.

The patient price this system charges is, on the later press account of the same talks, very low. De Standaard on 9 November 2026, in a snippet whose body was not retrieved, states that the GP remgeld is €4 and €1 for people with the verhoogde tegemoetkoming. A price that close to zero does not ask the patient to distinguish a contact that can wait from one that cannot. A higher ordinary copay, with the means-tested tariff left intact, is a standard way to cut low-value use without closing the door. Slowing the real growth norm does the other half of the same job: it stops the insurance budget from writing its own increase while the state is already in the excessive-deficit procedure. The alternative the partners used against the fallback — leave health whole and repair the path somewhere else — is how the index and the tax on labour become the residual every time.

There is also a baseline point that is not a cut in care. Nieuwsblad on 20 October, again in a snippet whose body was not retrieved, says new RVA figures show the number of long-term sick falling 9 percent by 2029 against earlier estimates, and that N-VA, MR and CD&V read that as a reason to keep going. If part of the "saving" is a revision of a disability forecast rather than a withdrawal of treatment, the headline overstates the care that disappears. The same snippet says the table holds €8.5 billion of savings and that about half comes from health, which is more than earlier notes. On the steelman, that share matches the share of the problem. Health is not a side budget.

The political reading is sequencing, not punishment. De Standaard on 20 October, snippet only, says the VAT yield in the previous table had already been cut to €500–700 million to keep MR, and that the prime minister looked to health to fill the hole. A coalition that will not tax consumption and will not touch the index has to open the health norm. Putting "meer dan 4 miljard" on that line, with a higher remgeld, a limit on the verhoogde tegemoetkoming, and a lower growth norm, is the proponent's way of saying the exemption is over. Partners can water the wine. They cannot honestly claim that a €10 billion path exists in which the insurance budget does not move.

## 2. Mechanism

Named mechanism(s):

1. **Growth norm.** The insurance budget rises by a statutory real norm plus indexation. A lower norm attacks that automatic increase directly. It does not, by itself, choose which care, which price, or which provider gives up the euro.
2. **Patient price.** A higher remgeld attacks a near-zero out-of-pocket price. That can cut contacts the patient does not value at the insurance tariff. It does nothing to fee-for-service on the supply side: the physician is still paid per act.
3. **Means-tested shield.** A limit on the verhoogde tegemoetkoming removes the instrument that was supposed to keep mechanism 2 from hitting the lowest incomes. Bundled with a higher remgeld, the shield and the price move in the wrong combination.
4. **Forecast revision.** A 9 percent lower path for long-term sickness, if the RVA snippet is right, is a baseline change in disability insurance. That is not the same mechanism as a GP copay. The extra €100 million the snippet attributes to the prime minister's note is a clawback of an activation yield that Vooruit disputes. It is not added here on top of the €4 billion, because no text says it sits outside that figure.
5. **Bargaining composition.** After the VAT line shrank, health is the plug for the parent €10 billion. The mechanism "make the envelope real" only works if the lines survive contact with the health minister and the mutualiteiten.

Does the instrument actually attack that mechanism?

Mechanism 1 can fit. A lower real growth norm is the lever that matches automatic budget growth. The Sunday texts that were read do not state the new percentage. "Lagere groeinorm" is a next-day snippet. A later De Standaard snippet (9 November) says the ambition to reach 3 percent above inflation by 2029 would be dropped back to the current 2 percent. That later sentence is not scored as the Sunday rate.

Mechanism 2 fits only as a design that was not published. No new remgeld amount appears in any text read here. The current €4 / €1 pair is a November snippet about the status quo, not the proposal.

Mechanism 3 is the part that fails. If the goal is to price low-value use while keeping care open, the verhoogde tegemoetkoming is the piece you keep. Cutting it converts a price signal into a transfer off the people the status was built for. Delayed care is a plausible dynamic cost. It is not in the headline saving.

Mechanism 4 is unproven and possibly double-counted with programmes already in the parent path. A disputed €100 million is not a €4 billion mechanism.

Mechanism 5 did not fire. The same weekend, three of five coalition parties said the table was not a basis. A composition nobody will negotiate is not a consolidation.

**mechanism_fit (0–10):** **4.0**

## 3. Options considered

| ID | Option | One-line effect |
|----|--------|-----------------|
| A | Status quo | Leave the growth norm on its current path, leave remgeld and the verhoogde tegemoetkoming as they are, and keep arguing about the parent €10 billion with health exempt. |
| B | Abolish / deregulate | Abolish the real growth norm (no automatic real increase) and open the fee schedule so entry and price are not both administered. Harsher on the budget path than a "lower norm", and it removes the nomenclature that captures the residual. |
| C | Price / tax reform | Raise the ordinary copay only, keep the verhoogde tegemoetkoming tariff where it is, and fill the rest of the parent envelope with a written consumption-tax shift. The VAT sketch stays `prop_2026_de_wever_btw_hervorming`. |
| D | Conditional transfer | A published rule that trims the least-targeted health reimbursements only when a stated deficit path is missed, with the verhoogde tegemoetkoming kept whole and a sunset. |
| E | Public provision / invest | Take the effort inside prices the federal insurer already sets: drugs, supplements, nomenclature, appropriate-care clauses. Show the lines that stay. Do not use the patient shield as the plug. |
| F | Pilot | One nomenclature chapter or one region, higher ordinary copay, verhoogde tegemoetkoming unchanged, pre-registered GP and emergency rates for that group, stop if access falls. |
| G | **This proposal** | The Sunday table's health line: headline €4 billion, described in next-day snippets as a higher remgeld, a narrower verhoogde tegemoetkoming, and a lower growth norm, inside the search for €10 billion. |

A is the do-nothing option on this instrument. B is the abolish option on the growth norm itself. G is what was put on the table.

## 5. Distribution & transfer constraint

- Winners: The federal budget, if the line is real, and taxpayers who would otherwise fund the parent path. Parties that want health to stay whole do not win on this table. MR wins only in the indirect sense that a smaller VAT line is what the Standaard snippet says this health plug replaced. That VAT euro is not re-scored here.
- Losers: People who lose verhoogde tegemoetkoming or pay a higher remgeld. Providers and mutualiteiten if the growth norm is the part that actually sticks. The health minister's existing programmes, if the disputed €100 million is clawed back.
- Exit / entry risk for productive base: A copay does not tax wages. A growth-norm fight that ends in lower fees can push physicians toward supplements or out of convention, which is an exit from the insured tariff, not from the country. No text retrieved measures that exit.
- New permanent clientele? The table does not create a subsidy clientele. It threatens an existing one. The verhoogde tegemoetkoming is already large. De Specialist on 12 November 2026, in a snippet whose page was not read in full, says a RIZIV report counted 2,508,094 beneficiaries at the end of 2025, almost 100,000 more than a year earlier and almost 20 percent more than at the end of 2021 (about 2.1 million). A status of that size is hard to cut cleanly; the compromise tends to exempt the loudest cases and leave a blunter copay.

**capture_risk (0–10):** **7.0**

Mutualiteiten, medical syndicates, and the verhoogde tegemoetkoming status will bargain the exceptions. An unpublished norm cut is captured by whoever is in the room. The patient price is the residual.

## 7. Belgian competence & implementation

- Competent level: Federal. The RIZIV/INAMI budget, the real growth norm, remgeld, and the verhoogde tegemoetkoming are federal social-security instruments. A budget table of the kern is the right room. It is not a regional decree.
- EU constraint: The parent path sits under the excessive-deficit procedure and an expenditure norm, on the account already used for `prop_2026_de_wever_10mrd_2029`. EU law does not prescribe this copay or this cut in the verhoogde tegemoetkoming. It is pressure on the total, not a design of the health line.
- Admin capacity / dual-structure friction: Insurance is federal. A large part of care organisation (prevention, elderly care, parts of hospital infrastructure) sits with communities and regions. A federal norm cut does not reform that supply. The health minister is Vooruit's Frank Vandenbroucke, whose party called the table unacceptable, so the administrative owner of the budget is the political veto. That is coalition friction, not a competence error.

**competence_fit (0–10):** **7.0**

## 9. Recommendation

`amend`

Minimum fix before this line is worth supporting: publish the bridge from the headline €4 billion to lines and to a year (2027 level, 2029 level, or a sum). Do not finance it by narrowing the verhoogde tegemoetkoming. If the ordinary remgeld rises, keep that status on today's tariff. Take the residual from the growth norm only against a published price and nomenclature list. Sunset the extra effort if the stated deficit path is met. A pilot (option F) is the honest version of the copay until those lines exist.

Rejecting the whole idea would exempt the automatic health norm from a path the same government says is €10 billion. That exemption is option A, and it is the option that keeps failing.
