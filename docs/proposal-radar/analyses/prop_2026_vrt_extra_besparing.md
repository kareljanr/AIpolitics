## Steelman (proponent’s best case)

Flanders is in the largest savings round a Flemish government has put on the table. The September declaration says the round reaches almost €2 billion in 2027 and €3 billion by 2029, of which €1.2 billion sits in government operations and subsidies. A public broadcaster with a dotation on the order of €300 million cannot be the one large organisation waved through while every other department is told to choose. The minister’s line on 12 October is that the extra VRT effort is no longer negotiable, because reopening it for a joint delegation of unions, management and the culture sector would make the budget a bargaining chip.

The euro path, as the press repeats it, is small next to that dotation and next to the Flemish total. The grant falls by €3.4 million next year and the cut reaches €7 million by 2029. Operating funds are not indexed. VRT itself puts the whole additional effort near €10 million by 2029. On a broadcaster the press still describes as costing €44.58 per Fleming a year, and as not among the most expensive public broadcasters in the EU, this is a trim, not a closure. The same minister says the core remit stays essential: independent journalism, explanation, a quality offer for young people, Flemish stories, culture and sport.

The way she says the trim should be filled is the abundance case. Exclusive contracts for on-screen faces pay a public premium to keep talent off other Flemish channels. Large sports rights, football and Francorchamps in her 12 October examples, Formula 1 and men’s football in the BRUZZ interview, are auction prices. She says the viewer should be able to watch, and that it matters less whether that is on VRT, VTM or Play. Sport the market will not carry, her example is women’s basketball, stays a public job. Entertainment may continue when it is distinct from commercial television, which the management agreement already requires. In exchange, VRT may build a paid formula above a VRT MAX base that stays free, broad and universal, and VRT asked for that permission itself. People who want more can pay. The taxpayer stops winning bidding wars.

She also answers the “you are cutting a starved broadcaster” claim with her own arithmetic: at the start of this legislature VRT was written €20 million extra, and across the management agreement another €55 million still comes. The €16.6 million path already in that agreement, and the €25 million transformation finished at the end of 2025, are earlier efforts, not a reason to exempt the house from this round. On the steelman, sharp choices at Reyerslaan are what “everyone contributes” means, and shutting the door on a second negotiation is how a budget stays a budget.

## 1. Problem claim

- **Metric / baseline:** Diependaele’s September declaration (30 Sep 2026, official publication) states a savings round of almost €2 billion in 2027 and €3 billion by 2029, of which €1.2 billion in government operations and subsidies. The speech text, searched for VRT, omroep and dotatie, does not itemise the broadcaster. The VRT slice is the press cluster: dotation −€3.4 million in 2027, −€7 million by 2029, operating funds not indexed, VRT’s total near €10 million, on top of a €16.6 million path for 2026–2031 and a €25 million plan VRT says it finished at the end of 2025. VRT NWS (30 Sep) says last year’s management agreement set an annual dotation of €296.4 million, still to be indexed. That agreement PDF was not extracted for this memo.
- **Who is hurt:** Flemish taxpayers if consolidation is delayed and interest or later cuts land harder; VRT staff and audiences if the residual cut hits news, youth and Flemish production; viewers if premium sport leaves Flemish screens entirely; commercial channels if the public broadcaster keeps bidding against them with tax money.
- **Type:** state failure (Flemish primary balance) mixed with a public-remit problem (tax money in exclusivity and premium rights). It is also a transfer fight over who inside the Flemish budget is exempt.
- **Truth of problem diagnosis (0–10):** **6.5**
- **Confidence:** **Strong** that Flanders has named a multi-billion consolidation. **Medium** that a few million on the VRT dotation is a coherent share of that round. **Weak** on the claim that VRT was spared and is now simply “paying its turn”: the stacked €25 million and €16.6 million are in the same articles, and the +€20 million / +€55 million counter-claim is one interview.
- **Sources:** Septemberverklaring PDF; VRT NWS 12 Oct 2026 (this unit); VRT NWS 30 Sep 2026 (NL and FR); BRUZZ 30 Sep 2026. No 2027 budget article, no minister’s communiqué PDF, no management-agreement extract.

The diagnosis that the Flemish budget needs a real effort is in a primary speech. The diagnosis that this VRT envelope is the necessary piece of that effort, and that the house was outside earlier efforts, is only partly supported.

## 2. Mechanism

Named mechanism(s):

1. **Across-the-board consolidation.** A cash cut in the dotation, growing from €3.4 million in 2027 to €7 million in 2029, plus a freeze on the indexation of operating funds, so one large public organisation shares the round.
2. **Public-remit refocus.** Stop paying for exclusivity over on-screen talent. Be more critical in auctions for expensive sports rights. Leave commercially viable sport to VTM, Play or a shared bid. Keep journalism, Flemish production, culture, education and sport the market under-provides.
3. **Voluntary price on the extra layer.** Permission to design a paid formula above a free, universal VRT MAX, at VRT’s request, so heavy users can fund more without a higher dotation. The base offer stays free, and programmes may not sit only behind the paywall.
4. **Commitment device.** The euro figures are closed. Unions, management and culture representatives do not get a second negotiation. Allocation inside the broadcaster is discussed with management and the board.

Does the instrument actually attack that mechanism?

The dotation path attacks (1) if the budget law books it. The index freeze attacks (1) only as foregone index, and its euro size in these sources is VRT’s sum, not a separate appropriation. Mechanism (2) is the one that would raise the quality of the saving, and on 30 September it is what the minister asks for. On 12 October the sports piece is still a piste, and the locked object is the number, while the filling is a conversation with management and the board. Multi-year rights and exclusivity contracts can outlast the first cash year, so the 2027 €3.4 million can land on staff and commissions before a rights contract expires. Mechanism (3) is permission. No source books a euro of subscription revenue against the €7–10 million. Mechanism (4) protects the number from a renegotiation cartel. It does not choose the margin that is cut.

**mechanism_fit (0–10):** **6.0**

## 3. Options considered

| B | Abolish / deregulate | Abolish exclusivity and the public bid for premium sport (men’s football, Formula 1, Francorchamps) and drop those tasks from the agreement, without a further cash cut to news, youth and Flemish production. Talent can work across Flemish channels. |
| C | Price / tax reform | Price the commercial layer: a paid tier above a free base, with the expected net revenue published and, if real, substituted for part of the dotation cut. No third use of an unmeasured subscription euro. |
| D | Conditional transfer | Keep the dotation level and condition the margin: claw back money that still goes to exclusivity premiums and premium rights; protect a measured floor for journalism, youth and Flemish production. |
| E | Public provision / invest | Hold the real dotation for the public remit and take this few million inside the €1.2 billion operations-and-subsidies effort somewhere the output is weaker. The September declaration already says education is spared; this option is explicit about that choice. |
| F | Pilot | One cycle only: share or drop the next expiring premium-rights contract, end new exclusivity, publish the euro saved, and test the paid tier without moving the free base. Scale the dotation path to the outturn. |
| G | **This proposal** | Lock dotation −€3.4 million in 2027 rising to −€7 million by 2029, plus non-indexation that VRT puts near €10 million in total, refuse to renegotiate, steer toward less exclusivity and fewer expensive sports rights, allow a paid tier above free VRT MAX. |

A is the signed contract. B is the cleaner instrument: it hits the rent the minister names and it changes the task list in the same decision as the money. G locks the money first and leaves the task list and the cut list for later.

## 5. Distribution & transfer constraint

- Winners: the Flemish budget, by a few million a year if the cut is cash-real; commercial broadcasters that no longer face a tax-funded exclusive bidder for faces and some sports rights; viewers, if those rights move to VTM or Play and stay on a Flemish screen; taxpayers, by a very small amount each.
- Losers: VRT staff, who struck on 12 October and describe workload and wellbeing; on-screen talent who lose an exclusivity premium; rights holders who lose a public bidder (a lower rights price is the point of the mechanism); independent producers and audiences if commissions, regional news, youth or minority sport are the residual; the credibility of a management agreement signed for 2026–2030 and reopened on the money before the tasks.
- Exit / entry risk for productive base: the productive base here is journalistic capacity and Flemish production, not a traded firm. Exit risk is that capacity leaving while already-signed rights stay. Entry that would help is commercial channels picking up rights the public broadcaster drops. A paywall that migrates the public remit would be entry in the wrong place.
- New permanent clientele? The cut itself shrinks one. The paid tier can grow a subscriber and platform clientele inside the public broadcaster. Refusing the renegotiation refuses a joint union–management–culture claim on the number. The same minister told BRUZZ she spared Brussels institutions in her other portfolio (Huis van het Nederlands, Muntpunt). That is a distribution choice beside this instrument, and it weakens the sentence that the strongest shoulders simply pay.

**capture_risk (0–10):** **5.0**

The proposal attacks two classic rents, talent lock-in and premium sports auctions, and it refuses to let a renegotiation coalition unwind the number. The internal cut list stays with the organisation, which can protect overhead and already-signed vehicles. The exclusivity ban is specified in politics before the euro schedule is specified in contracts. The paid tier is a new clientele seed if the free base is allowed to thin.

## 7. Belgian competence & implementation

- Competent level: Flemish Community. Media, the VRT dotation and the beheersovereenkomst sit there (Mediadecreet, the 18 Jul 2025 decision page for the 2026–2030 agreement). Federal and the other communities are the wrong level for this grant.
- EU constraint: public-broadcasting state aid and the rule that a commercial side must not quietly become the public remit. A free universal base plus an ancillary paid extra is the usual shape. These articles contain no state-aid note. Programmes may not, on the minister’s stated rule, sit only behind the paywall. That sentence is the guardrail, and it is still a quote.
- Admin capacity / dual-structure friction: VRT can implement a cut of this size. The friction is contractual and social. The agreement runs to 2030; VRT has asked for an addendum; the minister has closed the number and left the filling to management and the board. A strike is not an implementation incapacity. A task list that stays whole while the grant shrinks is the implementation defect.

**competence_fit (0–10):** **8.0**

## 9. Recommendation

`amend`

Minimum fix, before this is supportable:

- Publish two lines: the dotation path (€3.4 million in 2027, €7 million by 2029) separate from the index freeze, with the freeze’s euro shown as a function of the index, not as a round €10 million cash cut.
- Addendum to the 2026–2030 agreement in the same decision: drop or share the premium-rights and exclusivity tasks, and name a floor for journalism, youth and Flemish production that the €7 million may not consume first.
- Date the rights schedule to contract expiry so 2027 cash is not silently taken from the newsroom while football is still under contract.
- Keep the free universal VRT MAX base in the agreement text. Any paid tier stays ancillary. Do not book unpaid subscription revenue against the saving. Add the state-aid sentence the press accounts skip.

Direction of the saving and of the exclusivity/rights test can stand. The unlocked composition and the mid-contract task list cannot.
