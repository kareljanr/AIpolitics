## Executive judgment (fair)

Fighting real tax and social fraud is **compatible with abundance and fairness**: compliant workers and firms should not subsidise cheaters. Extra BBI/audit capacity, data matching, and clean procedure can have high ROI.

What is broken here is the **budget instrument**, not the moral claim. Booking **€300m in 2026** and **>€1.2bn over the legislature** as if enforcement were a cash machine — while the minister **delays the comprehensive plan**, coalition partners escalate, Inspectie Financiën questioned draft legal certainty and yield, and BBI reported yields fell ~**one third** (press, May 2026) — is **classic Belgian consolidation fiction**. Same family as optimistic dynamic effects and “camel” CGT yields.

Score is **mixed-negative**: principle **support-leaning**, packaging **amend hard / reject as standalone savings line**.

## Steelman (extensive, good faith)

### 1. Tax gap is a real free-rider problem

- Fraud and aggressive non-compliance raise the **effective wedge** on honest labour and SMEs who cannot hire planning armies.  
- Closing genuine gaps is closer to **efficiency** than raising rates on already-taxed bases.  
- Horizontal equity: two similar abilities-to-pay should not diverge because one hides.

### 2. Capacity is a binding constraint

- Regeerakkoord: **300** extra people across BBI, social fraud, judicial police, justice — multi-agency stack matches how complex fraud works (tax + social + criminal).  
- Jambon public line (2025): large share toward tax inspection. More eyes + specialised units can raise hit rates if selection is risk-based, not political.

### 3. Legal tools need modernisation

- General anti-abuse / specific schemes rot when case law and procedure lag.  
- A **Directie Fiscale Onderzoeken**-style framework (ministerraad path Jun 2026, press) can clarify who investigates what — steelman: less overlap, faster files.

### 4. Consolidation needs *some* revenue story without pure tax hikes

- Arizona must find large structural savings/revenue toward EU paths.  
- Well-executed enforcement is politically and economically preferable to another labour-tax hike or prestige spend.  
- Time-boxing a plan into the autumn budget can force a package rather than drip-feed decrees.

### 5. Delay can be quality, not sabotage

- Steelman for delay: rushing IF-criticised text creates court losses and **negative** net yield. Better a hard autumn package than a fragile spring law.

## 9. Recommendation

**`amend`** (hard).

Minimum fix package:

1. **Unbook or haircut** the 2026 €300m line to an IF/CoA-compatible range until measures are law.  
2. Publish a **one-pager instrument stack**: hires by entity, IT/data matching, legal changes, expected yield bands, lag.  
3. Fund the **300** with visible cadre budget — no unfunded “we will hire.”  
4. KPI dashboard: assessments, recoveries, court win rate, time-to-case — quarterly.  
5. Prefer **risk-based + simplify TE surface** over maximalist general anti-abuse that tanks legal certainty.

Default: enforcement **support in principle**; **reject** as a substitute for real spending cuts.
