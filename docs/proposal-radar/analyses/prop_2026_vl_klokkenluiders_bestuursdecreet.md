## Executive judgment (fair)

The **official instrument** (Crevits nota) is mostly boring-good administration: simplify messy reporting channels, make Audit Vlaanderen the external channel for its perimeter, free Ombuds capacity for a **central support desk**, add **criminal-range sanctions** for retaliation/obstruction/false reports (aligned with federal private/public regime), annual core reporting, extend scope to GO! central admin + Xperta, tighten identity confidentiality (including wrong channel).

The **“doofpotdecreet”** attack (unions / green NGOs / Knack) is not pure invention either: stronger confidentiality and “professional treatment” rules can be **weaponised** to narrow what becomes public under openbaarheid van bestuur, exactly when whistleblowing surfaces waste or capture — which is this project’s DOGE-adjacent interest.

Fair score: **not circus**, **not genius**. Support the channel/support/sanction package; **amend hard** any drafting that lets secrecy of *wrongdoing facts* ride on secrecy of *whistleblower identity*.

## Steelman (extensive, good faith)

### 1. EU duty + evaluation cycle

- Directive (EU) 2019/1937; Flanders transposed 18 Nov 2022 into Bestuursdecreet.  
- Art. 14: competent authorities must evaluate procedures at least every three years.  
- Visienota 18 Jul 2025 + consultation → this decree is the legal follow-through, not a random gag law.

### 2. Channel complexity is a real barrier

- Current landscape confuses potential reporters.  
- Clear split: **internal** = head of entity; **external** = Audit Vlaanderen (its perimeter) or Vlaamse Ombudsdienst (rest).  
- Dropping Audit as *internal* channel matches Directive logic: head of entity best placed to fix fast.

### 3. Support desk is the missing piece

- Directive expects free information/advice on remedies, retaliation protection, rights of reporter and accused.  
- Ombuds as support service uses freed capacity when Audit takes half the external perimeter (~staff-weighted).

### 4. Sanctions need teeth

- Current admin/disciplinary only; Directive + OECD anti-bribery Phase 4 Belgium pressure for effective deterrence.  
- Proposed: **6 months–3 years** and/or fine **€4,800–€48,000**, *wetens en willens* only — not forgetfulness.

### 5. Coverage holes (GO! admin, Xperta)

- Closing gaps where staff fell between education-sector rules and Bestuursdecreet is integrity hygiene.

### 6. Confidentiality of identity is not optional

- Identity secrecy is core whistleblower protection; incomplete transposition on wrong-channel recipients was already flagged by Raad van State.  
- Steelman: without identity protection, rational silence → more unreported fraud.

### 7. Fiscal neutrality claim is refreshing

- “Within existing means,” IF green light, no extra headcount narrative — rare honesty vs “new agency + millions.”

## 9. Recommendation

**`amend`**

Keep:

1. Channel simplification + support desk  
2. Intentional retaliation/false-report sanctions  
3. Scope gaps GO!/Xperta  
4. Annual core reporting  

Must-fix:

1. **Explicit non-regression clause for openbaarheid:** whistleblower *identity* protected; **facts of alleged public wrongdoing** remain FOI-eligible under ordinary Bestuursdecreet tests.  
2. Publish annual **anonymised outcome** stats (upheld / not / referred / sanctions), not only intake counts.  
3. Independent 3-year review with civil-society seat on evaluation (Directive spirit).

Reject only if final text uses confidentiality to blanket-block FOI on public money files.

---

## 10. Falsifier

> We reverse toward **reject** if within 24 months of entry into force FOI/appeal practice shows a **material drop** in released documents on public-money irregularities where the sole cited ground is klokkenluiders-confidentiality **beyond** identity protection, **or** if bona fide report volumes fall while retaliation complaints rise without sanction use.

> We reverse toward **support** if reporting quality/outcomes improve and FOI win rates on substance hold steady or rise.
