## Steelman (proponent’s best case)

Belgium has already learned what an unfinished budget feels like. If the Chamber has no budget for the new year, the state falls onto provisional twelfths: each month the government may spend about one twelfth of last year’s credits. VRT’s account is plain. Those credits are last year’s figures. They are not rewritten for this year’s prices, this year’s interest bill, or this year’s obligations. The prime minister’s public line, reported across that week, is that he will not accept 2027 opening on that automatic pilot.

His answer, as VRT described it on 15 October, is to agree the emergency package in advance. Put about €3 to €4 billion of 2027 savings on the shelf. In essence, freeze government expenditure, with some exceptions still unnamed. If the multi-year deal to 2029 is closed in time, the shelf stays shut. If it is not, the package can be switched on — N-VA’s example was December — so the country has a chosen 2027 budget instead of a mechanical copy of 2026. Parliament would still have to speak. The kern was supposed to decide within a day or two. On this telling, the first goal does not move: the agreement through 2029 remains the real prize, and the package is only the net under the tightrope.

The market half of the steelman is the part the label is reaching for. A political crisis that leaves the budget unsigned can be read by bond investors as a reason to charge more. A higher rate on a large debt stock feeds the next deficit. A pre-agreed freeze is meant to be the sentence that stops that reading: the Belgian state has already decided what it will not spend if the talks break. VRT is explicit that the Swiss and German constitutional brakes are a different object. Those bind expenditure to long-run revenue, or cap the structural deficit, and they sit in a constitution. Changing the Belgian constitution needs a two-thirds majority, which this coalition does not have and which this proposal does not seek. The steelman is narrower and more honest than the nickname. Call it a backstop, not a Schuldenbremse. A backstop can still be the adult move when the alternative is to sleepwalk into twelfths and hope the spread does not notice.

The composition, in the version Vooruit was already describing to VRT on 15 October, is mostly non-indexation: health budgets, civil-service pay, benefits, pensions. That is crude, and the steelman should admit it. Indexation is automatic, so not doing it is one of the few levers that produces billions without inventing a new tax administration in a weekend. A freeze also falls on the state’s own wage bill and on transfer budgets the federal level actually controls. It does not require MR to swallow a general VAT rise in the same week, which is why critics said the design seated MR in an armchair. From the prime minister’s side that is a feature. A backstop that includes the coalition’s hardest taboo will never be ready in time. A backstop made of decisions the federal budget can execute is a backstop that can exist.

There is a second, colder steelman inside the “poison pill” description VRT published on 19 October. If the government falls, the caretaker period should not be a holiday from consolidation. A law that is ready, and that the partners know will bite pensions and wages, raises the cost of walking away. Parties that refuse both the multi-year table and the backstop then own the twelfths. That is leverage, and leverage is how a five-party cabinet finishes a budget. The plan was still being worked out technically on 15 October. The claim is not that every line was wise. The claim is that a specified €3 to €4 billion for 2027, written down before the crisis, dominates both silence and a copied budget.

(2) The same article: provisional twelfths release about **one twelfth per month of the previous year’s budget**, unadjusted to new needs. (3) VRT, 19 Oct 2026: the kern communiqué, quoted there, stops the stability-law discussion and turns back to a multi-year effort of **€10 billion**. A De Standaard line that the effort is “more than the Commission needs” is a snippet from a page this pass could not open. It is not used as a number.
- **Who is hurt:** Residents who live with twelfths or with a higher debt-service bill if the 2027 budget is simply missing. If the freeze is the one Vooruit described, also civil servants, benefit recipients, pensioners, and patients whose health budget would have been indexed. Who sits inside the “exceptions” is not published. Bondholders gain if the spread stays down. That gain is the proposal’s hope, not a measured transfer.
- **Type:** state failure in the budget process (a missing statute replaced by a mechanical twelfth), inside a real consolidation fight. The incidence of the freeze is a transfer fight. The market-snowball story is a hypothesis about price, not a documented failure in the sources read here.
- **Truth of problem diagnosis (0–10):** **7**
- **Confidence:** **Medium** that provisional twelfths are a poor 2027 default and that the coalition was, that week, trying to land a multi-billion consolidation. **Weak** on whether €3 to €4 billion is the right 2027 slice, on whether a pre-agreed freeze would move the spread, and on the claim that the multi-year deal stays easier once a smaller backstop exists. Partners said the opposite, and they said it before the kern dropped the plan.
- **Sources:** VRT NWS 15 Oct 2026 (Stefan Grommen), read in full. VRT NWS 19 Oct 2026 (Ivan De Vadder), read in full, including the kern sentence and the Mahdi quotations. HLN 14 Oct 2026 headline only.

The diagnosis that Belgium should not enter 2027 on a copied budget is in good shape. The diagnosis that this unfinished freeze was the way to secure both 2027 and the path through 2029 is not carried by the sources.

## 2. Mechanism

Named mechanism(s):

1. **Mechanical default.** With no budget statute, monthly credits track last year. A pre-agreed 2027 package replaces that default with a chosen cut.
2. **Indexation as the automatic spend.** Health budgets, civil-service pay, benefits and pensions rise when prices rise, unless a decision stops them. Skipping that rise is how the press accounts produce billions without a new tax.
3. **Market expectation.** A visible backstop is supposed to stop investors from pricing a political crisis into the rate, and so to stop a rate snowball. No spread is cited.
4. **Coalition leverage.** VRT’s 19 Oct account calls the stability law a poison pill: in a political crisis the heavy measures would switch on, and parties that fear an index skip would prefer to stay at the table. CD&V and Vooruit read the same design as a gift to MR, because the hard measures for 2028 and 2029 could then wait.
5. **Label.** “Schuldenrem” points at a Swiss or German constitutional rule. VRT says the proposal does not seek a constitutional amendment and could not get a two-thirds majority on this timetable.

Does the instrument actually attack that mechanism?

Mechanism 1 is a real fit if the package is voted and is specific enough to be a budget. On 15 October it was still “technisch uitgewerkt”. A freeze “with some exceptions”, exceptions unlisted, is not yet a budget a treasury can execute. Mechanism 2 fits the arithmetic of non-indexation, and it is the content Vooruit and, later, Sammy Mahdi actually attacked. Mahdi’s line on 19 October, in an interview given before the Saturday kern and reported by VRT, is that the measures are “een regelrechte aanval op de index van gepensioneerden en het loon van werkende mensen”. That is a blunt instrument. It does reduce expenditure relative to an indexed baseline. It does not choose low-value spend inside health, and it does not touch the tax mix.

Mechanism 3 is untested here. A backstop moves a spread only if investors believe it would be activated and would bind. A package the partners had already called unacceptable, and which the kern then parked, is a weak signal. Mechanism 4 cuts against mechanism 1. A pill that is credible as punishment may bring partners back to the €10 billion table. The same pill, once it is the only 2027 decision on offer, lets the coalition leave 2028 and 2029 unsolved. Mahdi’s other sentence is the mechanism in one line: if they find only €4 billion for 2027, 2028 and 2029 become very hard. The kern’s own communiqué chose the multi-year table and stopped the stability-law talk. The instrument’s political design fought its stated sequencing.

Mechanism 5 is a naming failure. The thing that could have been scored as a one-year expenditure rule was scored in public as a debt brake. That invites a comparison it loses, and it hides the incidence. Vooruit’s word was non-indexation. The nickname was Schuldenbremse.

**mechanism_fit (0–10):** **4.5**

## 5. Distribution & transfer constraint

- Winners: the federal treasury, if the freeze binds and the exceptions do not eat it. Future taxpayers, to the extent a real €3 to €4 billion slows the debt. MR, if the critics are right that a 2027-only decision delays the measures that party dislikes for 2028 and 2029. That political win is not a euro.
- Losers: civil servants, pensioners, and benefit recipients whose index would be skipped, on Vooruit’s and Mahdi’s public reading. Health-budget users, if “niet-indexeringen van gezondheidsbudgetten” means volume or prices rather than a paper reserve. People inside the unnamed exceptions are spared. They cannot be listed from these sources.
- Exit / entry risk for productive base: a civil-service pay freeze makes federal recruitment harder in any job the private sector will pay to index. A health-budget freeze can show up as waiting time, which is a real capacity cut, or as a shift to privately paid care. Neither effect is measured here. The package contains no supply-side leg.
- New permanent clientele? No new subsidy and no new agency. The capture path is the exceptions list, still blank on 15 October, and the crisis trigger, which pays the party that holds out.

**capture_risk (0–10):** **5**

## 9. Recommendation

`reject`

Do not revive this fallback. A 2027 budget that is only a freeze of unnamed index lines, switched on because the coalition has failed, is a worse statute than either a voted multi-year table or an honest set of twelfths. The problem the steelman names — do not copy last year’s credits by accident — survives this rejection. It belongs to the €10 billion track the kern reopened on 17 October. That track is a different instrument and is not scored in this row.

If a later text is tabled and someone wants an amend path, the minimum fix is option F plus a published exception list: one budget year, lines named, cash audited, no crisis trigger, and no constitutional nickname.
