## Executive judgment (fair)

Regional airports that need permanent subsidy are a **structural DOGE surface**: private jets, training flights, thin commercial use, endless “strategic for the port” rhetoric. The summer package *claims* lower support 2025–29 (€96m vs €131m) — if true, a partial win on the envelope.

The **dading** is the clown core:

1. **Secret** commercial peace while parliament is sold a “no blank cheque / more autonomy” story.  
2. **Triple Inspectie van Financiën negative** opinions (press; minister disputes “ignored”).  
3. Mutual claim wipe: Flanders drops a **~€12.1m staff claim**; operator drops subsidy arrears claims (after a **2024 judgment** that already hurt Flanders on part of the dispute).  
4. Locks **subsidy agreements to October 2039** — multi-legislature capture.  
5. Leak only because of a **cabinet document error** — process competence failure on top of substance.

Steelman litigation-risk is real. It does **not** justify secrecy + IF thrice-no + 2039 lock. **Reject** the dading as governance and fiscal design; re-open transparency and IF compliance path.

## Steelman (extensive, good faith)

### 1. Litigation was real and already going badly

- Disputes since **~2019**.  
- **2024 judgment** put Flanders in the wrong on part of subsidy issues (minister account via VRT).  
- Continuing court war = legal fees + interest + risk of full Egis claim (~€14.8m with PFAS/VMM/permit cost add-ons).  
- Settlement can be rational *expected-value* management.

### 2. Mutual write-off is not a one-way gift on paper

- Flanders claimed ~€12.1m for **seconded staff**.  
- Egis claimed unpaid promised subsidies + extras.  
- Net of two uncertain claims can be closer to zero than opposition “cadeau” framing.

### 3. Closing history enables a “cleaner” future contract

- Minister: settle history → new subsidy agreements with **stricter** conditions, less room for new disputes.  
- Regeerakkoord line: further **verzelfstandiging**, lower dependence — summer envelope cut narrative (€96m vs €131m) fits that steelman.

### 4. Operational roles exist beyond holiday jets

- Antwerp business aviation / training / niche links near port complex; Ostend has cargo/niche roles (steelman, not proven CBA).  
- Abrupt shutdown has transition and local political costs.

### 5. Confidentiality in commercial settlements is sometimes lawful

- Steelman: litigation strategy and commercial terms can be sensitive.  
- (Still fails democratic control when IF is thrice negative and parliament is blindsided.)
