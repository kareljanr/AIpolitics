## Steelman (proponent’s best case)

The federal government is still looking for about €10 billion to put the budget back on a path it can defend. That figure is De Tijd’s frame for the whole search, and it is the room in which this menu appears. Inside that search, the argument from Les Engagés is that the next euro should come from financial capital, so that the savings asked of health care can be smaller. De Tijd’s lede says the list of capital-tax increases and new capital taxes is mainly meant to push those health-care savings down. A universal health-insurance budget is a real service. If the alternative on the table is a deeper cut there, a visible contribution from asset owners is the fairness claim that lets the coalition keep the cut smaller.

The menu, as the secondary press describes it, does not invent a new tax administration from scratch. It turns dials on taxes the Arizona government has already built. The capital-gains tax has been in force since 1 January 2026 at a standard 10 percent on realised gains on financial assets, with a €10,000 yearly exemption and a step-up for historical gains at 31 December 2025 (law of 6 April 2026, as summarised by the ITAA blog on 9 September 2026). Banks already sit in the withholding chain. The securities-account tax (effectentaks) has just moved from 0.15 percent to 0.30 percent for reference periods ending on or after 1 June 2026, above a €1 million average (programme law of 30 May 2026, as summarised by ABN AMRO MeesPierson). The stock-exchange transaction tax (beurstaks / TOB) and an exit tax already exist. Using those pipes is faster than designing a new wealth tax in the two days before the prime minister’s deadline.

The piece with the cleanest equity story is the registered-share extension. People who keep securities on an account above €1 million pay the effectentaks. People who convert the same securities into registered shares step outside the account. The anti-abuse rules already tell banks to report those conversions. Extending the tax to registered shares would tax the position itself, so the shelter stops being a planning product. A tighter exit tax is the complement: a higher domestic rate that people can leave behind in a weekend does not tax the mobile base, it taxes the people who stay. Doubling or raising the new capital-gains rate, in this telling, is still a tax on a realisation, so it avoids the annual valuation of unsold shares that a wealth tax requires.

Les Engagés also have a negotiation reason to put a long list down. 21 News says the party treats the items as options on the table, and that they are neither an agreement nor necessarily final demands. Two days before a deadline, a menu is how a junior coalition partner forces the prime minister’s “best and final” to contain a capital slice large enough to show their voters. The press floor attached to that slice is at least €1 billion (21 News) or more than €1 billion (v-nieuws). The party can say it will not sign a consolidation that is only expenditure cuts after labour and unemployment have already been in earlier rounds.

That is the best case. It depends on three things the leak does not show: a base large enough to yield the floor, a definition that actually hits liquid financial wealth, and a health-care euro that gets spared because this euro arrives.

## 1. Problem claim

- **Metric / baseline:** De Tijd’s lede (1 Nov 2026) places the list inside a search for €10 billion. That number is the government’s consolidation problem, and it is not a yield claimed for this menu. No Court of Audit table, no FPS Finance costing, and no health-care savings line in euros were retrieved for the cuts this menu is supposed to shrink. The capital-gains tax Prévot wants to raise is ten months old: standard rate 10 percent from 1 January 2026, historical gains stepped up at 31 December 2025, yearly exemption €10,000, and a separate progressive scale for a substantial holding of at least 20 percent with an exemption up to €1 million (ITAA blog, 9 September 2026, describing the law of 6 April 2026). The effectentaks is 0.30 percent above €1 million from reference periods ending on or after 1 June 2026, and registered shares held off an account stay outside the base (ABN AMRO, 7 August 2026).
- **Who is hurt:** Under the proponent’s frame, patients and providers who would bear a larger health-care saving, and coalition MPs who think capital has contributed too little relative to labour. Under the instrument as leaked, holders of financial assets, people who realise gains, people who trade, people who leave, and family shareholders if registered shares of a private company enter the effectentaks.
- **Type:** transfer fight, with a real state-failure backdrop (a large structural deficit). The “broadest shoulders have not been touched” diagnosis is a preference claim laid on top of taxes that have already been created or doubled in 2026.
- **Truth of problem diagnosis (0–10):** **4.5**. The consolidation gap is a real public-finance problem. The claim that this menu is the residual fair share is weakly specified: health-care savings are not quantified in the sources read, and the capital base has already been reopened twice this year (new capital-gains tax; effectentaks rate doubled).
- **Confidence:** Weak on the composition of the menu and on the euro floor. Medium on the legal baseline of the 10 percent capital-gains tax and the 0.30 percent effectentaks, which several professional explainers describe the same way. The statute PDFs were not opened this tick.
- **Sources:** De Tijd lede; HLN via Nieuwskoppen; 21 News; v-nieuws (direct fetch failed; search extract used); ITAA blog; ABN AMRO MeesPierson.

## 2. Mechanism

Named mechanism(s):

1. **Transfer.** Extra receipts from capital, so a health-care saving can be smaller. This is the stated point of the list (De Tijd lede).
2. **Realisation tax.** A higher capital-gains rate taxes the act of selling. People who need liquidity sell; people who can wait, wait. The step-up at 31 December 2025 means the taxable slice in 2026 is new gain only. A rate increase on a young, stepped-up base is a thin tax on a timing choice.
3. **Transaction tax.** A higher beurstaks taxes turnover. It hits rebalancing and funds that trade, including small holders if no exemption is specified. The leak specifies no exemption, no instrument list, and no cap.
4. **Shelter closing.** Putting registered shares inside the effectentaks attacks the conversion path out of the securities-account tax. That path is already an anti-abuse target when the conversion is done to escape the tax. Shares that were always registered, which is the ordinary way to hold a family company, were outside because they are not an account balance. Taxing them requires a yearly value for a firm that does not trade.
5. **Mobility backstop.** A tighter exit tax raises the cost of changing tax residence after the domestic rate rises. Without a published deferral, threshold, or holding period, it is a lock on the way out.
6. **Legal-form surcharge.** “Extra charges on management companies” (v-nieuws; 21 News says the taxation of some management or service companies is also mentioned). Belgian practice uses ordinary companies for professional labour. There is no separate corporate form under that name. A surcharge needs a definition the leak does not give. Aimed at capital, it can land on incorporated work.

Does the instrument actually attack that mechanism?

The health-care mechanism is political, not designed. No source read here names the saving that gets smaller, the budget article, or the euro-for-euro link. A menu that “can” raise money does not, by itself, protect a hospital budget. The prime minister can take the capital line and keep the health-care line.

The capital-gains piece attacks realisations, which is a legal tax handle Belgium has just built, and it does so before the first filing cycle has produced a yield. The ITAA blog notes that banks could not withhold the tax on gains realised from January through May 2026, and that those gains fall to the tax return unless the holder opted into voluntary withholding by 31 August 2026. Raising the rate in November 2026 means changing the price before that return data exists. v-nieuws, citing De Tijd, calls the move a doubling of the rate. 21 News says only “an increase”. Neither text says whether the standard 10 percent, the substantial-holding scale, the €10,000 exemption, or the 31 December 2025 step-up is what moves. A doubling, if it means the standard slice, is a rate claim with no base.

The beurstaks piece is a weak match for “broadest shoulders”. Transaction taxes collect from people who trade. Patient holders of a family firm pay none of it. The effectentaks extension matches the shelter story for listed securities that were moved off an account, and it misses that story for unlisted shares that were never on one. 21 News flags the family-company and SME point directly. The exit-tax piece matches flight risk only after someone publishes the parameter that is being tightened. The management-company piece has no mechanism until the draft says who is in.

**mechanism_fit (0–10):** **3.5**. One real base-closing idea (registered shares that are a conversion shelter) is bundled with rate hikes, a transaction tax, an undefined exit-tax turn, and an undefined company surcharge, and none of them is tied to a named health-care euro.

## 3. Options considered

| ID | Option | One-line effect |
|----|--------|-----------------|
| A | Status quo | Keep the 10 percent capital-gains tax, the 0.30 percent effectentaks, the current beurstaks and the current exit tax. Let the first capital-gains filing cycle finish. Find the €10 billion elsewhere in the conclave. |
| B | Abolish / deregulate | Drop this menu. Do not raise a tax that is ten months old. The deregulatory twin, if the goal is domestic capital formation, is to leave rates alone and let the existing anti-abuse rules work on conversions, rather than creating a yearly tax on unlisted registered shares. |
| C | Price / tax reform | One instrument, one base, a published static yield and a behavioural band. | D | Conditional transfer | Spare a named health-care article only euro-for-euro against yield that FPS Finance has already collected, after one full assessment year. No advance credit for a political floor. |
| E | Public provision / invest | If the harmed party is health care, publish the service that would be cut (fee schedule, hospital envelope, reimbursement) and run a spending review on that envelope. A capital menu does not choose a therapy. |
| F | Pilot | Freeze every rate. Publish, for the 2026 assessment year, realisations, effectentaks receipts, and conversion volumes. Pilot registered-share inclusion only for listed names that left an account after a stated date. |
| G | **This proposal** | An uncosted multi-tax menu (higher or doubled capital-gains rate, higher beurstaks, tighter exit tax, effectentaks on registered shares, possible management-company charges) offered as options worth more than or at least €1 billion, so health-care savings can be smaller. |

A, B, C, D and F are all available before G. G skips the measurement the 2026 taxes were supposed to produce. C is the amend path if a later text isolates the conversion shelter. B is the live alternative for this leak: do not legislate a menu that has no base.

## 5. Distribution & transfer constraint

- Winners: The health-care envelope, if and only if a later deal actually reduces a named saving because this revenue exists. Les Engagés and, on the press account, Vooruit, who need a “broadest shoulders” line in the conclave. The treasury, to the extent any slice survives behaviour and carve-outs.
- Losers: People who realise 2026-and-after gains on the slice whose rate rises. People who pay beurstaks, including anyone who rebalances, unless a later text carves them out. Holders of registered shares, with the sharpest bite on unlisted family companies if the extension is not limited to listed securities that left an account. Anyone who changes tax residence, if the exit tax gets tighter. Professionals who work through a company, if “management company” is defined broadly.
- Exit / entry risk for productive base: A higher realisation tax plus a tighter exit tax is a pair that punishes both selling and leaving. New investment can be booked outside Belgium before it ever sits on a Belgian account. Family firms that cannot sell a slice of shares to pay an annual account-style tax face a liquidity problem the effectentaks was built to avoid by sticking to accounts. That is an entry tax on staying incorporated in Belgium.
- New permanent clientele? The tax itself does not create a subsidy clientele. It does create a revenue dependency and a valuation bureaucracy if unlisted registered shares must be priced every year. The health-care constituency that is “bought off” by the tax becomes a reason to keep both the tax and the spending. Carve-outs (substantial holding, historical step-up, small-account beurstaks caps, start-up exemptions) are the usual capture path, and the 2026 capital-gains tax already has several of them.

**capture_risk (0–10):** **6.0**. High enough to matter. The bargain is a permanent capital tax in exchange for sparing a spending line, negotiated by the parties who need the fairness token, with the technical carve-outs still unwritten.

## 7. Belgian competence & implementation

- Competent level: Federal. Capital-gains tax, beurstaks, effectentaks and exit tax are federal instruments. The health-care insurance budget this menu wants to spare is also settled in the federal conclave. A regional decree cannot do this package.
- EU constraint: An exit tax on unrealised gains has to stay proportionate to free movement, which in practice means a real deferral for moves inside the EU, not a cash bill on the day of departure. The leak publishes no deferral, no threshold, and no holding period, so compliance cannot be checked. The effectentaks on residents’ foreign accounts and on non-residents’ Belgian accounts is already a designed federal tax; extending it to registered shares adds a valuation problem, not a new level of government.
- Admin capacity / dual-structure friction: Withholding banks can change a capital-gains rate if the slice is one they already touch. They cannot value an unlisted family company four times a year. The capital-gains tax is not through its first ordinary assessment: January–May 2026 gains were outside withholding. Rewriting the rate during that first cycle is an implementation choice the finance administration has not been given a text for. “Management company” has no code heading until a draft writes one. Language-community friction is low; the friction is inside the federal coalition. MR and N-VA are the parties the press says balked. Les Engagés are in the same government. That is a whip problem, not a competence problem.

**competence_fit (0–10):** **5.5**. Right level of government. The objects that would actually raise the difficult money (unlisted registered shares, exit tax, undefined companies) are the objects this leak does not specify.

## 9. Recommendation

`reject`

Reject the menu as a budget instrument. A conclave can still do option C later, as its own proposal: effectentaks on listed shares converted to registered form to avoid the €1 million threshold, with a published yield and a behavioural band, and with no rate change on the capital-gains tax until the 2026 assessment year has a receipt. That text is not what was leaked on 1 November 2026.

Minimum change that would move this row from reject to amend: one instrument, a draft article, a base, a rate, a static yield, a one-paragraph dynamic effect, and the health-care article whose saving shrinks by that yield.
