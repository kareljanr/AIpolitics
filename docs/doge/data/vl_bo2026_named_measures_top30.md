## Honesty

- Centenboekje is a **political summary**, not a full L5 third-party register.  
- Full named subsidy lists remain FOI-adjacent (CJSM, VLAIO, etc.).  
- Negative figures are **budget deltas**, not negative spending.  
- Do **not** sum all rows into 66 bn (double-count domain totals + measures).
