**No invented euros.** Federal −25% cut is **policy path** (coalition 2025–29) unless budget article cash is public.

## What Unia is

Interfederal independent public body (cooperation agreement **12 Jun 2013**, art. 16 financing). Mandates discrimination/equality for federal + participating federated entities. **Flanders stopped financing Unia from 2023** and runs **Vlaams Mensenrechteninstituut (VMRI)** instead → dual NL/FR equality architecture.

### Policy path 2025–29 (medium–strong process, not yet full cash)

Federal coalition agreement: **reduce Unia financing by 25%**. Unia 2024/2025 reports confirm impact on operational plan/staff.  
Illustrative only if applied to 2025 federal line: 0.75 × €8,304,698 = **€6,228,524** — **not booked as fact** until BGD/outturn.

## Dual structure: Flanders exit → VMRI

| Entity | Role | Public budget class |
|--------|------|---------------------|
| Unia | Interfederal (without Flanders) | Subsidies ~€9.5–9.6m (2024–25) |
| **VMRI** | Flanders-only human rights / equality body | VP docs: **2025 ~€5.279m** total budget class; **2026 request ~€5.598m** (pers. €4.48m + working €1.118m) — medium–strong parliament docs |

Mechanism: Flanders replaced its Unia contribution (historically ~€1m class secondary press) with a **standalone multi-million VMRI** — dual overhead pattern (same policy domain, two institutions).
